# Stock Photo Shortlist

Candidate images for the site. All are from **Unsplash** (free license) or **Pexels** (free license) — both allow free commercial use without attribution (credit appreciated). Neither allows selling the photos unaltered or implying endorsement by people shown.

**Before launch:** download the chosen images and serve them from our own storage/CDN (resized/WebP via Next.js `<Image>`), rather than hotlinking.

## ⚠️ Gap: interiors of a modern 2-bedroom + loft trailer

Free stock has **no** consistent set of a new US-style bunkhouse travel trailer (2 bedrooms, loft, kitchen). Results are mostly European caravans, camper vans, or vintage campers. The interior photos below work for **mood/lifestyle sections only**. For the camper detail pages, options are:

1. **Photograph our own trailers** (recommended — customers book the exact unit; misleading interior photos create disputes).
2. **Manufacturer/dealer marketing photos** — only with written permission.
3. **Paid stock** (Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, iStock — search "bunkhouse travel trailer interior").
